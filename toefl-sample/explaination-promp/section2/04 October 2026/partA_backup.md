Question 1
Why (B)?

Cholesterol, a primary component of many biological membranes, is essential for cell function.

💡 QC C.O.R.E ANALYSIS

C - Concept : Subject & Appositive (Subjek & Keterangan Tambahan)

O - Observe S-V-C :

Sentence Formula Classification: SVC

Visual Sentence Breakdown:
Subject ( _____ ), Complement (a primary component of many biological membranes), Verb (is), Complement (essential for cell function).

Textual Observations:

• Subject : [KOSONG/BLANK]

• Verb : is (Sudah ada)

• Complement : Frasa , a primary component of many biological membranes, di antara dua koma adalah Appositive.

R - Requirement:
Kalimat ini sudah memiliki Verb (is) namun belum memiliki Subject. Karena tidak ada Connector, kita hanya membutuhkan satu Noun/Noun Phrase sebagai Subject. Dilarang menambahkan Verb ekstra.

E - Eliminate:

(A) It is cholesterol ✕ : Terdapat Subject ekstra It dan Verb is yang menyebabkan Double Verb tanpa konektor.

(B) Cholesterol ✓ : Noun tunggal yang berfungsi sempurna sebagai Subject.

(C) Cholesterol is ✕ : Memiliki Verb is, menyebabkan Double Verb.

(D) That cholesterol ✕ : Penggunaan konektor That membuat klausa ini menjadi Dependent Clause sehingga kalimat kehilangan Main Clause.

Question 3
Why (B)?

Not until the late nineteenth century did electricity become widely available to the general public.

💡 QC C.O.R.E ANALYSIS

C - Concept : Negative Inversion (Inversi akibat Negative Expression)

O - Observe S-V-C :

Sentence Formula Classification: SVC

Visual Sentence Breakdown:
Complement (Not until the late nineteenth century), Verb ( _____ ), Subject ( _____ ), Complement (widely available to the general public).

Textual Observations:

• Subject : [KOSONG/BLANK]

• Verb : [KOSONG/BLANK]

• Complement / Pengecoh : Not until the late nineteenth century adalah Negative Expression di awal kalimat.

R - Requirement:
Ketika Negative Expression (Not until) diletakkan di awal kalimat, struktur kalimat di posisi rumpang harus di-inversi: Auxiliary Verb + Subject + Main Verb (Bare Infinitive).

E - Eliminate:

(A) electricity became ✕ : Susunan normal (Subject + Verb), tidak mengikuti aturan inversi.

(B) did electricity become ✓ : Menggunakan urutan inversi yang tepat: Auxiliary (did) + Subject (electricity) + Verb Base (become).

(C) became electricity ✕ : Melakukan inversi langsung Main Verb tanpa Auxiliary Verb (did).

(D) electricity did become ✕ : Posisi Auxiliary did berada setelah Subject, bukan di depannya.

O - Observe S-V-C :

Subject (Mount Everest), Complement ( _____ over 8,800 meters), Verb (is), Complement (the highest mountain in the world).
