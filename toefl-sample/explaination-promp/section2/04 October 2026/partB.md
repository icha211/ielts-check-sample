C - Concept : Correlative Conjunctions (Kata Hubung Berpasangan Baku)

O - Observe S-V-C :

Sentence Formula Classification: SVC

Visual Sentence Breakdown:
Subject (Neither the regional manager _____ the office employees), Verb (were), Complement (completely aware of the upcoming policy changes).

Textual Observations:

Pasangan baku Correlative Conjunction:

Either berpasangan dengan or

Neither berpasangan dengan nor

E - Eliminate:

(B) were ✓ : Verb plural mengikuti subjek terdekat (office employees).

(C) upcoming ✓ : Present participle berfungsi sebagai adjective menerangkan policy changes.

(D) changes ✓ : Plural noun yang menjadi objek dari preposisi of.
