# Question 1
## Why (B)?

Cholesterol, a primary component of many biological membranes, is essential for cell function.

---

### 💡 QC C.O.R.E ANALYSIS

#### **C - Concept:** 
Subject & Appositive (Subjek & Keterangan Tambahan)

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

<span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**Cholesterol**</span>, a primary component of many biological membranes, <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**is**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**essential for cell function**</span>

**Tag Labels:**
- <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 8px; border: 1px solid #D1DCE8; border-radius: 4px; font-size: 12px;">Subject</span>
- <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 8px; border: 1px solid #D1DCE8; border-radius: 4px; font-size: 12px;">Verb</span>
- <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 8px; border: 1px solid #D1DCE8; border-radius: 4px; font-size: 12px;">Complement</span>

##### Textual Observations:

• **Subject:** Cholesterol (Noun tunggal sebagai Subject utama)

• **Verb:** is (Sudah ada)

• **Complement:** Frasa "a primary component of many biological membranes" di antara dua koma adalah Appositive. Frasa "essential for cell function" adalah Complement utama.

#### **R - Requirement:**

Kalimat ini sudah memiliki Verb (is) dan Subject (Cholesterol) yang lengkap. Subject berupa Noun tunggal yang berfungsi sempurna. Karena tidak ada Connector, struktur ini merupakan Main Clause. Dilarang menambahkan Verb ekstra atau Subject tambahan.

#### **E - Eliminate:**

(A) It is cholesterol ✕ : Terdapat Subject ekstra "It" dan Verb "is" yang menyebabkan Double Verb tanpa konektor.

(B) Cholesterol ✓ : Noun tunggal yang berfungsi sempurna sebagai Subject.

(C) Cholesterol is ✕ : Memiliki Verb tambahan "is", menyebabkan Double Verb.

(D) That cholesterol ✕ : Penggunaan konektor "That" membuat klausa ini menjadi Dependent Clause sehingga kalimat kehilangan Main Clause.

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

<span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**Found in the deepest parts of the ocean**</span>, <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**certain species**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**thrive**</span> under extreme pressure and in complete darkness.

**Tag Labels:**
- <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 8px; border: 1px solid #D1DCE8; border-radius: 4px; font-size: 12px;">Modifier</span>
- <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 8px; border: 1px solid #D1DCE8; border-radius: 4px; font-size: 12px;">Subject</span>

• **Subject:** certain species (Noun Phrase yang terletak setelah Modifier)

• **Verb:** thrive (Sudah ada)

• **Modifier:** "Found in the deepest parts of the ocean" adalah Past Participle Modifier di awal kalimat.

#### **R - Requirement:**

(A) thriving of certain species ✕ : Tidak memiliki Main Verb; thriving di sini adalah participle.

(D) when certain species thrive ✕ : Menggunakan subordinator when yang menciptakan Adverbial Clause tanpa adanya induk kalimat.

# Question 3
## Why (B)?

Not until the late nineteenth century did electricity become widely available to the general public.

---

### 💡 QC C.O.R.E ANALYSIS

#### **C - Concept:**
Negative Inversion (Inversi akibat Negative Expression)

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

<span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**Not until the late nineteenth century**</span>, <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**did**</span> <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**electricity**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**become**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**widely available to the general public**</span>

##### Textual Observations:

• **Subject:** electricity (Noun tunggal)

• **Verb:** did become (Auxiliary + Main Verb dalam pola Inversion)

• **Modifier:** "Not until the late nineteenth century" adalah Negative Expression di awal kalimat.

#### **R - Requirement:**

Ketika Negative Expression (Not until) diletakkan di awal kalimat, struktur kalimat di posisi rumpang harus di-inversi: Auxiliary Verb + Subject + Main Verb (Bare Infinitive).

#### **E - Eliminate:**

(A) electricity became ✕ : Susunan normal (Subject + Verb), tidak mengikuti aturan inversi.

(B) did electricity become ✓ : Menggunakan urutan inversi yang tepat: Auxiliary (did) + Subject (electricity) + Verb Base (become).

(C) became electricity ✕ : Melakukan inversi langsung Main Verb tanpa Auxiliary Verb (did).

(D) electricity did become ✕ : Posisi Auxiliary did berada setelah Subject, bukan di depannya.

Prior to the invention of the printing press, books were copied by hand and were extremely expensive.

---

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

<span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**Prior to**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**the invention of the printing press**</span>, <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**books**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**were copied**</span> by hand and <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**were**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**extremely expensive**</span>.

##### Textual Observations:

• **Subject:** books (Plural Noun)

• **Verb:** were copied dan were (Auxiliary + Main Verb dalam struktur pasif)

• **Prepositional Phrase:** "Prior to the invention of the printing press" adalah Modifier di awal kalimat yang membutuhkan preposition.

#### **R - Requirement:**

Kalimat utama sudah lengkap dengan Subject dan Verb. Bagian rumpang membutuhkan kata depan (Preposition) yang tepat untuk melengkapi Noun Phrase menjadi Prepositional Phrase / Modifier.

(A) It was prior to ✕ : Menambahkan Subject (It) dan Verb (was) ekstra yang berlebihan.

(B) Prior ✕ : Berfungsi sebagai Adjective, memerlukan pasangan preposition to jika diikuti oleh Noun Phrase.

(C) Prior than ✕ : Idiom yang salah; ungkapan yang benar adalah "Prior to", bukan "Prior than".

(D) Prior to ✓ : Prepositional phrase majemuk yang tepat bermakna "sebelum".

# Question 5
## Why (B)?

The widespread use of pesticides and herbicides poses a significant threat to local water supplies.

---

### 💡 QC C.O.R.E ANALYSIS

#### **C - Concept:**
Subject-Verb Agreement & Prepositional Distractor

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

<span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**The widespread use**</span> of pesticides and herbicides <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**poses**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**a significant threat to local water supplies**</span>

##### Textual Observations:

• **Subject:** The widespread use (Singular Noun - pengecoh adalah "of pesticides and herbicides")

• **Verb:** poses (Singular Verb)

• **Complement:** "a significant threat to local water supplies" (Object dari Verb)

#### **R - Requirement:**

Subjek kalimat adalah kata tunggal "use" (bukan "pesticides and herbicides"). Oleh karena itu, posisi rumpang membutuhkan Singular Verb (Verb-s/es) untuk Present Tense.

#### **E - Eliminate:**

(A) pose ✕ : Merupakan Plural Verb (untuk subjek jamak).

(B) poses ✓ : Merupakan Singular Verb yang sesuai dengan subjek tunggal use.

(C) posing ✕ : Participle (Verb-ing) tidak bisa menjadi Main Verb tanpa to be.

(D) are posing ✕ : Menggunakan Auxiliary plural are, tidak cocok dengan subjek tunggal.

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

<span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**It**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**is**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**a generally accepted scientific theory**</span> <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**that an asteroid impact**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**caused**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**the extinction of the dinosaurs**</span>

(C) why ✕ : Digunakan untuk alasan/sebab, tidak cocok dalam mendefinisikan teori fakta.

# Question 7
## Why (A)?

A typical modern orchestra is composed of string, woodwind, brass, and percussion instruments.

---

### 💡 QC C.O.R.E ANALYSIS

#### **C - Concept:**
Parallel Structure (Struktur Pararel/Sejajar)

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

<span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**A typical modern orchestra**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**is composed of**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**string, woodwind, brass, and percussion instruments**</span>

##### Textual Observations:

• **Subject:** A typical modern orchestra

• **Verb:** is composed of (Passive Voice)

• **Complement:** Deretan kata sifat/kata benda penjelas sebelum instruments: string, woodwind, brass, and [...]

#### **R - Requirement:**

Penggunaan kata penghubung "and" menuntut struktur yang sejajar (Parallel Structure). Kata "string", "woodwind", dan "brass" semuanya bertindak sebagai Noun Adjunct. Maka bagian rumpang harus berupa Noun tunggal pemodifikasi.

#### **E - Eliminate:**

(A) percussion ✓ : Bentuk Noun sejenis yang sejajar dengan string, woodwind, brass.

(B) they are percussion ✕ : Merusak paralelisme karena memasukkan Subject dan Verb baru.

(C) playing percussion ✕ : Menggunakan Participle (playing) yang tidak setara dengan kata-kata sebelumnya.

(D) percussionists ✕ : Berarti "pemain perkusi" (orang), bukan jenis instrumen musiknya.

# Question 8
## Why (C)?

The more oxygen a living organism consumes, the more energy it produces.

---

### 💡 QC C.O.R.E ANALYSIS

#### **C - Concept:**
Double Comparative (Perbandingan Ganda)

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

<span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**The more oxygen**</span> <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**a living organism**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**consumes**</span>, <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**the more energy**</span> <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**it**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**produces**</span>

##### Textual Observations:

• **First Clause Subject:** a living organism

• **First Clause Verb:** consumes

• **Second Clause Subject:** it

• **Second Clause Verb:** produces

#### **R - Requirement:**

Pola Double Comparative wajib mengikuti rumusan sejajar: The + comparative (+ Noun) + S + V, The + comparative (+ Noun) + S + V. Bagian rumpang membutuhkan pola "The + comparative + Noun".

#### **E - Eliminate:**

(A) more energy ✕ : Kehilangan article the di depannya.

(B) energy is more ✕ : Memasukkan Verb is yang salah letak dan merusak pola komparatif.

(C) the more energy ✓ : Mengikuti pola baku the + comparative + noun dengan tepat.

(D) that the more energy ✕ : Menambahkan konektor that yang tidak diperlukan.

# Question 9
## Why (D)?

Mount Everest, whose peak is over 8,800 meters, is the highest mountain in the world.

---

### 💡 QC C.O.R.E ANALYSIS

#### **C - Concept:**
Possessive Relative Clause (Klausa Relatif Kepemilikan)

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

<span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**Mount Everest**</span>, <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**whose peak**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**is**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**over 8,800 meters**</span>, <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**is**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**the highest mountain in the world**</span>

##### Textual Observations:

• **Main Clause Subject:** Mount Everest

• **Relative Clause:** Dimulai dengan "whose peak is over 8,800 meters" adalah Adjective Clause non-restrictive

• **Main Clause Verb:** is

#### **R - Requirement:**

Bagian rumpang memerlukan kata ganti relatif kepemilikan (Possessive Relative Pronoun) untuk menyatakan kepemilikan puncak (peak) dari Mount Everest, diikuti oleh Verb penjelasnya.

#### **E - Eliminate:**

(A) which peaks ✕ : Menggunakan peaks sebagai kata kerja, mengubah maknanya secara keliru.

(B) its peak is ✕ : its adalah possessive pronoun baku, tidak bisa menghubungkan dua klausa (run-on sentence).

(C) the peak of which is ✕ : Secara tata bahasa mungkin, tetapi terlalu kaku dan kurang tepat dibandingkan kepemilikan langsung.

(D) whose peak is ✓ : whose secara sempurna menyatakan kepemilikan (whose peak = puncak milik Gunung Everest) disusul verb is.

# Question 10
## Why (B)?

To ensure the structural integrity of the bridge, engineers conduct routine inspections every six months.

---

### 💡 QC C.O.R.E ANALYSIS

#### **C - Concept:**
Infinitive of Purpose (To-Infinitive Tujuan)

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

<span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**To ensure**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**the structural integrity of the bridge**</span>, <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**engineers**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**conduct**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**routine inspections every six months**</span>

Kalimat utama sudah utuh dengan Subject (engineers) dan Verb (conduct). Bagian rumpang di awal kalimat berfungsi menyatakan tujuan tindakan (purpose), yang membutuhkan To-Infinitive (To + Verb 1).

#### **E - Eliminate:**

# Question 11
## Why (A)?

Had they more time, the research team would have been able to complete the clinical trials.

---

### 💡 QC C.O.R.E ANALYSIS

#### **C - Concept:**
Inverted Conditional Sentence (Pengandaian Inversi Type 3)

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

<span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**Had**</span> <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**they**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**more time**</span>, <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**the research team**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**would have been**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**able to complete the clinical trials**</span>

##### Textual Observations:

• **Conditional Clause:** Dimulai dengan "Had they more time" (Inversi dari If they had)

• **Main Clause Subject:** the research team

• **Main Clause Verb:** would have been

#### **R - Requirement:**

Kalimat ini menggunakan pola Conditional Type 3 (would have been). Jika kata penghubung "If" dihilangkan (omission of IF), maka klausa syarat di bagian rumpang harus di-inversi menjadi: Had + Subject + (Verb 3/Object).

#### **E - Eliminate:**

(A) Had they ✓ : Inversi tepat dari If they had (had).

(B) If they have ✕ : Tense salah (Present Tense have tidak cocok dengan klausa utama would have been).

(C) They had ✕ : Klausa independen biasa tanpa konektor If atau inversi, salah secara tata bahasa.

(D) Have they ✕ : Tense salah (Present Tense).

# Question 12
## Why (D)?

When dropped in a vacuum, all objects fall at exactly the same rate regardless of their mass.

---

### 💡 QC C.O.R.E ANALYSIS

#### **C - Concept:**
Reduced Adverbial Clause (Peringkasan Klausa Pasif)

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

When <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**dropped**</span> in a vacuum, <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**all objects**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**fall**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**at exactly the same rate regardless of their mass**</span>

##### Textual Observations:

• **Reduced Clause:** "dropped in a vacuum" adalah Past Participle (Verb 3) dalam bentuk ringkas

• **Main Clause Subject:** all objects

• **Main Clause Verb:** fall

#### **R - Requirement:**

Setelah konjungsi waktu (When), klausa dapat diringkas (reduced). Karena objek (all objects) menerima perlakuan/pasif ("ketika dijatuhkan"), maka bentuk ringkasan di rumpang adalah Past Participle (Verb 3).

#### **E - Eliminate:**

(A) are dropped ✕ : Tidak ada Subject (they) setelah When.

(B) dropping ✕ : Present Participle (Verb-ing) bermakna aktif ("ketika menjatuhkan"), salah secara logika.

(C) they drop ✕ : Bermakna aktif ("ketika mereka menjatuhkan").

(D) dropped ✓ : Past Participle (Verb 3) pasif ringkas dari When (they are) dropped.

Piloting a commercial jet requires a high level of concentration and physical endurance.

---

### 💡 QC C.O.R.E ANALYSIS

#### **C - Concept:**
Gerund Phrase as Subject (Gerund sebagai Subjek)

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

<span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**Piloting a commercial jet**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**requires**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**a high level of concentration and physical endurance**</span>

##### Textual Observations:

• **Subject:** "Piloting a commercial jet" adalah Gerund Phrase

• **Verb:** requires (Singular Verb untuk Subject singular)

• **Complement:** a high level of concentration and physical endurance

#### **R - Requirement:**

Kalimat sudah memiliki Verb singular (requires). Diperlukan sebuah Subject berbentuk kata benda (Noun/Gerund Phrase) di posisi rumpang.

#### **E - Eliminate:**

(A) Pilot a commercial jet ✕ : Pilot di awal adalah Verb 1 (Imperative/kalimat perintah), tidak bisa menjadi Subject.

(B) Piloting a commercial jet ✓ : Gerund phrase (Verb-ing + object) berfungsi sempurna sebagai Subject singular.

(C) A commercial jet piloted ✕ : Menjadikan jet sebagai subjek yang diikuti participle, mengubah makna kalimat secara tidak logis.

(D) To piloting a commercial jet ✕ : Bentuk tata bahasa salah (To preposition tidak bisa langsung bertemu Verb-ing untuk fungsi ini).

# Question 15
## Why (C)?

Despite the heavy rain, the outdoor graduation ceremony proceeded as planned.

---

### 💡 QC C.O.R.E ANALYSIS

#### **C - Concept:**
Contrast Preposition vs Conjunction (Preposisi Pertentangan)

#### **O - Observe S-V-C:**

##### Visual Sentence Breakdown:

<span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**Despite the heavy rain**</span>, <span style="background-color: #0EA5E9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**the outdoor graduation ceremony**</span> <span style="background-color: #30B0C7; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**proceeded**</span> <span style="background-color: #E675C9; color: #FFFFFF; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 3px;">**as planned**</span>

##### Textual Observations:

• **Subject:** the outdoor graduation ceremony

• **Verb:** proceeded

• **Prepositional Phrase:** "Despite the heavy rain" adalah kontras preposisi di awal kalimat

#### **R - Requirement:**

Kata "the heavy rain" hanya berupa Noun Phrase (tanpa Verb). Maka posisi rumpang memerlukan Preposition (Kata Depan) pertentangan, BUKAN Conjunction (Kata Hubung Klausa).

#### **E - Eliminate:**

(A) Although ✕ : Merupakan Conjunction, harus diikuti oleh klausa lengkap (Subject + Verb).

(B) In spite ✕ : Tidak lengkap; bentuk prepositional yang benar harus menggunakan of (In spite of).

(C) Despite ✓ : Merupakan Preposition yang langsung diikuti Noun Phrase.

(D) Even though ✕ : Merupakan Conjunction, wajib diikuti klausa utuh (Subject + Verb).
