## Question 1

**Correct Answer:** C - The math building is located near the library.

**EXPLAINATION** 
The second speaker says the building is “right across the street from the main library.” The phrase “across the street from” is paraphrased in the correct answer as “located near.” Therefore, the correct answer is C because it accurately restates the location she described using different words.

**WHY THE OTHER OPTION IS INCORRECT**
*   **(A) She is studying math at the library.:** This option is incorrect because it introduces a new context that is not mentioned in the audio. The speaker said “math” and “library” separately in the audio, but she did not say she is studying math at the library.
*   **(B) She doesn’t know where the building is.:** This option contradicts the audio. The speaker clearly knows where it is because she gives him specific directions.
*   **(D) The library is closed right now.:** This option introduces information that was completely unstated in the audio. There is no mention of the library being closed.

## Question 2
**Correct Answer:** A - Eat lunch together.

**EXPLAINATION** 
The woman asks if they are "still meeting for lunch" and the man confirms by saying "Yes," adding that he will see her at the "campus cafeteria." Meeting at a cafeteria for lunch means they are planning to share a meal. Therefore, the correct answer is A because it accurately describes their agreed-upon plan.

**WHY THE OTHER OPTION IS INCORRECT**
*   **(B) Skip their afternoon classes.:** This option is incorrect because it introduces a new context that is completely unstated in the audio. There is no mention of their class schedules or skipping classes.
*   **(C) Cook a meal at home.:** This option contradicts the audio. The man specifically states they will meet at the "campus cafeteria," which means they are eating on campus, not cooking at home.
*   **(D) Meet at the library.:** This option is incorrect because it gives the wrong location. The man explicitly says he will see her at the "cafeteria," not the library.
